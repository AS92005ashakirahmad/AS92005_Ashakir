RUBBER FIG

● HEALTH : EXCELLENT
Rubber Figs like indirect sunlight. Let the soil dry out between watering. You’ll know the plant is overwatered if the leaves turn yellow. Occasionally wipe off the leaves of the plant so they don’t get dusty.
