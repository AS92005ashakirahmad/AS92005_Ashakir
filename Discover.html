Kaitiakitanga & Deforestation

Sproutify promotes Kaitiakitanga by highlighting the devastating ecological impacts of deforestation and offering practical solutions for native reforestation. Being a kaitiaki (guardian) means actively defending tree canopies against destruction, rehabilitating degraded soil, and planting eco-sourced native saplings. By linking classroom learning to real-world conservation projects, MRGS students can exercise meaningful guardianship to ensure our native bush thrives for generations to come.
