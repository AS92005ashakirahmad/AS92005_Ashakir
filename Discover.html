Kaitiakitanga & Deforestation

Sproutify promotes Kaitiakitanga by raising awareness about the impacts of deforestation and encouraging native tree protection in New Zealand. Being a guardian of the environment means taking action against tree clearing, protecting wildlife habitats, and restoring native bush. Through local community actions and education, students can practice guardianship and help build a greener future.
