Kaitiakitanga

My website, Sproutify, connects to Kaitiakitanga because it teaches people to care for and protect nature. Kaitiakitanga means being a guardian of the environment and taking responsibility for living things. Sproutify helps users practise this by showing them how to grow plants, remember watering times, and understand the needs of their plants. By caring for plants, users can build a stronger connection with nature and learn that even small actions can help protect the environment.
