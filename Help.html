Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best native plants for beginners to grow at home in Auckland?

Harakeke (NZ Flax), Tī Kōuka (Cabbage Tree), and Koromiko are ideal for beginners. They are highly resilient, adapt quickly to various soil types, handle Auckland's weather conditions well, and rapidly attract native birds like Tūī and Silvereyes.

Why is planting native trees better than non-native exotic trees?

Native trees have co-evolved over millions of years with New Zealand’s native birds, lizards, and insects. Exotic trees (like pine or oak) often do not provide the correct food sources or nesting environments for native wildlife and can sometimes become invasive weeds that smother local bush.
