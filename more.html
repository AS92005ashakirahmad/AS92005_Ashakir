-MORE ABOUT DEFORESTATION-

1. Ancient Canopy (The Past)

Historically, vast native forests covered the landscape, providing shelter for unique wildlife and regulating the natural climate. These dense ecosystems acted as massive carbon sponges, balancing local weather patterns and preserving rich biodiversity for generations.

2. Deforestation & Today's Crisis

Rapid urbanization, agriculture, and industrial development have led to widespread forest clearing. Today, millions of hectares of native trees are lost annually, resulting in habitat loss, soil erosion, and accelerated climate change.

3. Restoring the Balance (Our Future)

Through Kaitiakitanga, we can reverse this trend. Planting native species, supporting local reserves, and making sustainable everyday choices allow communities to rebuild wildlife corridors and restore ecological harmony.
